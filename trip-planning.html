Trip Planning Recommendations

Use this page for any notes you want to share with guests about timing, bookings, and general tips for Angie’s 50th in Belize.

When to Book

  • Book international flights into Belize City (BZE) as early as you’re comfortable, especially if traveling on peak days.
  • Reserve your room at Coco Beach Resort (or Belizean Shores) once you know your dates.
  • Island hopper flights (Tropic Air / Maya Island Air) can be booked once flights to BZE are confirmed.

Golf Carts & Getting Around

  • Plan on renting a golf cart for your stay to get between the resort and San Pedro town.
  • Booking in advance is recommended if you’re arriving on busy days or later in the evening.

Packing Tips

  • Lightweight clothing, swimsuits, cover-ups, and something casual-nice for dinners.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, and water shoes or sandals that can get wet.
  • Snorkel gear is optional — operators usually provide it if you don’t pack your own.

Communication

  • Share your arrival details (flight into BZE and Tropic/Maya flight time) so the group has a sense of who’s arriving when.
  • Group text or WhatsApp is helpful once everyone is on the island.
