When to Book

  • Book international flights into Belize City (BZE) and reserve your room at Coco Beach Resort (or Belizean Shores) as early as you’re comfortable.
  • Island hopper flights (Tropic Air / Maya Island Air) can be booked once in Belize.

Golf Carts & Getting Around

  • Plan on renting a golf cart for your stay to get between the resort and San Pedro town (about a 20 minute golf cart ride).

Packing Tips

  • Lightweight clothing, swimsuits, cover-ups, and something casual-nice for dinners.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, and water shoes or sandals that can get wet.
  • Snorkel gear is optional — operators usually provide it if you don’t pack your own.

Communication

  • Share your arrival details (flight into BZE) to the group text so the group has a sense of who’s arriving when.
  • We'll want to use WhatsApp once everyone is on the island.
