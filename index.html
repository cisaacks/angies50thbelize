Getting There – Flights & Island Hop

You’ll fly into Belize City (BZE), then take a short island-hopper flight to Ambergris Caye.

Step 1 – Fly to Belize City (BZE)

Multiple airlines serve Belize City. However, American Airlines offers the quickest route to Belize from many U.S. cities.

Tip: Search from your home airport to “Belize City (BZE)” and look for routings that get you in on the afternoon of April 29, 2026.

American Airlines: aa.com

Step 2 – 15-Minute Flight to Ambergris Caye

Once you arrive in Belize City, you’ll connect to a short island-hopper flight up to San Pedro on Ambergris Caye.

Main option – Tropic Air: book directly for frequent daily flights between Belize City and San Pedro.

Tropic Air website

Additional option – Maya Island Air: alternative carrier with similar routes and timing.

Maya Island Air website

Book once in Belize after getting through Customs/Immigration. The flight is about 15 minutes and lands right in San Pedro town.
