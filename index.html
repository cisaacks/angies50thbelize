Getting There – Flights & Island Hop

You’ll fly into Belize City (BZE), then take a short island-hopper flight to Ambergris Caye.

Step 1 – Fly to Belize City (BZE)

Multiple airlines serve Belize City. However, American Airlines offers the quickest route to Belize from many U.S. cities.

American Airlines: aa.com

Step 2 – 15-Minute Flight to Ambergris Caye

Main option – Tropic Air: Direct flights Belize City → San Pedro.

Tropic Air website

Additional option – Maya Island Air: Similar routes/times.

Maya Island Air website

Book once in Belize after getting through Customs/Immigration. The flight is about 15 minutes.
